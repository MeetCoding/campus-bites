# Campus Bites — Privacy Policy

_Placeholder policy for V1. Last updated: 17 October 2026._

Campus Bites is the dining, mess, canteen and eatery app for IIT Roorkee. This page explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

## What we collect
- **Google account details** (for IIT Roorkee members): name, `@iitr.ac.in` email address and profile picture, received through Google Sign-In.
- **Phone number** (for whitelisted vendors and canteen staff only), used to sign in.
- **What you give us in the app**: your Bhawan, complaints and their attachments, mess rebate applications and supporting documents, and menu or noticeboard photos uploaded by staff.
- **Device push token**, used to send notifications such as mess timing changes and complaint updates.

## How we use it
- To sign you in and check that you are an IIT Roorkee member or an approved vendor.
- To show menus, timings and complaint boards, and to send complaints and rebate applications to the right Bhawan authorities.
- To send notifications about your complaints, rebates and your Bhawan's mess.

## Google user data
We use data from your Google account only to sign you in and to show your name and email inside the app. We do not use it for advertising, sell it, or share it with anyone outside the institute's dining administration.

## Storage and sharing
Data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, and processed by our API server. We share complaint and rebate details only with the institute officials responsible for them (Mess and Bhawan Secretaries, the Warden office, and ADoSW). The public complaint board does not show who raised a complaint. We do not sell personal data.

## Your choices
You can ask for your account and data to be deleted by opening an issue in this repository or contacting the Campus Bites team.
